01 // Behind the Mind

THE ALIVEINTERFACE

I'm an AI researcher, designer, and creator passionate about building tools that connect emotionally with people.

Every project I create — whether an AI-powered writing assistant or a gamified productivity tracker — is designed for impact, simplicity, and accessibility. My research focuses on the MAWO model (Mastery & Achievement Workflow Optimizer), bridging the gap between cold computational weights and the warmth of human behavioral narrative.

I am moving from static "click-response" paradigms to fluid, intent-based interactions. The "Black Box" of AI is not just a technical challenge — it's a design failure. My mission is to make intelligence transparent.
